Dental Healthcare • New Delhi

Multi-Chair Dental & Implant Center

Baseline: Ranking #8 to #14 on Google Maps outside a 500m radius. Receiving less than 15 phone calls per month from Google Maps despite having modern clinic facilities.

Actions Taken (60 Days):

Re-structured 14 primary/secondary medical categories, built high-intent service descriptions for implantology and orthodontics, uploaded geo-tagged clinic photos, and introduced a compliant QR review counter standee.

Home Services • Bengaluru

Multi-Brand AC & Appliance Repair

Baseline: Disputed location due to technician dispatch address. Visibility collapsed right as summer temperatures peaked across East Bengaluru.

Actions Taken (45 Days):

Converted to a policy-compliant Service-Area Business (SAB), hid residential premises, mapped 12 postal service zones, aligned website LocalBusiness schema, and launched weekly local update posts.

+210%

Hospitality • Jaipur

Heritage Boutique Hotel & Rooftop Cafe

Baseline: Sudden hard suspension with "Deceptive Content" flag after an unauthorized receptionist changed the pin location. Completely removed from Google Maps.

Actions Taken (14 Days):

Compiled a verified evidence package (GST registration, property lease, municipal trade license, video walk-through, high-res exterior signage) and submitted a structured formal reinstatement appeal.
