Personal Background & Philosophy

Helping Indian Businesses Win on Google Maps With Integrity.

A

Ankit

Google Business Profile & Local SEO Specialist

Independent Consultant • Remote All-India Service
✓ 50+ Profiles Handled ✓ 100% Policy-First ✓ Direct WhatsApp Access

I started working with Google Business Profiles after seeing how many hardworking Indian doctors, store owners, contractors, and entrepreneurs were losing business to inferior competitors simply because their Google Maps profiles were incomplete or poorly maintained.

Even worse, many had been burned by agencies promising "guaranteed #1 rankings" who stuffed fake keywords into business names or bought fake reviews—resulting in sudden policy suspensions that paralyzed their daily revenue.

My approach is completely different: I treat your Google Business Profile as an essential digital storefront that requires accurate governance, policy hygiene, and clear customer signals. When you work with me, you work directly with a specialist who understands Google's guidelines inside and out.

My Working Principles

1. Zero Black-Hat Tactics

No fake locations, virtual offices, keyword-stuffed business names, or fake reviews. We build sustainable, long-term prominence that protects your business from Google crackdowns.

2. Transparent Communication

You never have to guess what is happening. We communicate directly via WhatsApp and phone, explaining every change before it is implemented.

3. Safe Account Access

I will never ask for your Google account password. All work is conducted securely through official Google Business Manager invitations.

4. India-Wide Geographic Understanding

Serving clients remotely across Indian metros and tier-2 business hubs. Whether your target clientele is in Mumbai, Bangalore, Jaipur, or Lucknow, local search intent is accurately addressed.
