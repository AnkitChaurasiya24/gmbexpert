Setup FAQs

Common Questions About Profile Setup

For businesses in India, Google typically requires proof of physical existence: permanent exterior and interior signage, business registration documents (GST Certificate, Udyam, or Trade License), utility bills under the business name, and proof of access to the premises via video verification.

When video verification is conducted correctly with clean evidence, approval typically takes between 2 to 5 business days. In some cases, Google may request additional manual review.
