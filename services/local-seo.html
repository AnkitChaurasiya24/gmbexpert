The 3 Ranking Pillars

How Google Decides Local Maps Placement

According to Google's official documentation, local results are determined primarily by three factors. We optimize each systematically.

🎯

1. Relevance

How well your local business profile matches what the user is searching for. We ensure rich services, categories, and website content align with high-intent customer search queries.

📍

2. Distance (Proximity)

How far your business location is from the searcher's physical location. We optimize exact map pins and designated service areas to maximize discovery across your core neighborhoods.

⭐

3. Prominence

How well-known a business is in the real world and across the web. Built through genuine reviews, consistent directory citations, backlinks, and active customer engagement.
