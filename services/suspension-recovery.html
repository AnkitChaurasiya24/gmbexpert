Google retains sole authority over account suspensions and reinstatement decisions. No consultant can ethically guarantee reinstatement or bypass Google's automated systems. Root Causes

Common Reasons Google Suspends Profiles

Most Indian business listings are suspended not out of malice, but because of innocent edits that trigger automated security filters.

🚩 Address or Phone Edit

Editing the physical address, phone number, or business name without prior supporting documentation triggers immediate automated 'Deceptive Content' flags.

🚩 Business Name Keyword Stuffing

Adding city names, service keywords, or taglines to the title that do not match the company's real-world permanent storefront sign.

🚩 Co-Working or Virtual Office Flags

Using virtual addresses, P.O. boxes, or shared desk spaces without having dedicated permanent signage and staffed hours.
