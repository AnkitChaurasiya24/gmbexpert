What we do guarantee is 100% adherence to Google's official best practices, eliminating compliance risks and maximizing your profile's competitive prominence.

Never share your Google password with anyone. Google Business Profile allows you to add managers safely through your dashboard. You simply send an invitation to our official management email with 'Manager' access. You retain 100% primary ownership and can revoke access anytime with one click.

We conduct an exhaustive policy compliance audit to identify why Google flagged the listing (e.g. naming violations, mismatch between GST and signage, suspicious address changes). We then compile an authoritative evidence dossier and draft the formal appeal. While Google makes the ultimate decision, our policy-first submissions have successfully restored legitimate businesses.

Google only removes reviews that explicitly violate their Prohibited and Restricted Content policies (such as hate speech, harassment, fake engagement, or clear conflict of interest). We can report policy-violating reviews through Google's formal removal channels. For legitimate negative customer feedback, we draft professional, de-escalating responses and implement an ethical review acquisition system to build genuine positive authority.

Regular SEO focuses on ranking pages nationally or globally in traditional organic web results. Local SEO focuses on geo-specific searches with local intent (e.g. "pediatrician in Indiranagar" or "plumber near me"), aiming for prominent placement in Google Maps and the local "3-Pack" map preview where high-intent calls happen.
